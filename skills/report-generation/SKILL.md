# Vendor Onboarding Report Generation Skill

## Objective
Transform raw compliance verification data into clear, audit-ready summaries for procurement officers and professional notifications for vendors regarding missing items.

---

## Required Inputs
- `vendor_summary`: Output object generated by the `compliance-check` skill.
  - `vendor_id`: Unique identifier
  - `vendor_name`: Vendor trading name
  - `completion_percentage`: Numeric completeness score ($0-100\%$)
  - `missing_documents`: List of missing document names
  - `status`: Submission classification
- `recipient_role`: Either `"procurement_team"` (internal audit) or `"vendor_contact"` (external reminder).

---

## Procedure
1. **Validate Compliance Data**: Ensure inputs are non-empty and reflect valid `compliance-check` results.
2. **Determine Report Variant**:
   - For internal procurement teams: Build an executive table with risk metrics and required human actions.
   - For vendor contacts: Build a polite, unambiguous notification requesting the specific missing documents.
3. **Draft Executive Summary**:
   - Include submission date, vendor details, completeness score, and an itemized list of outstanding requirements.
4. **Attach Standard Compliance Disclaimers**:
   - Explicitly note that document presence does not constitute formal onboarding or contract approval.
5. **Output Clean Markdown**: Deliver the final rendered notification or table.

---

## Output Templates

### Internal Procurement Summary Template
```markdown
### Vendor Onboarding Audit Summary
- **Vendor Name**: [Vendor Name] (ID: [Vendor ID])
- **Status**: [READY_FOR_HUMAN_EVALUATION | INCOMPLETE_DOCUMENTS_PENDING]
- **Completeness Rate**: [XX.X]% ([S] of [R] documents submitted)
- **Outstanding Items**:
  - [Missing Item 1]
  - [Missing Item 2]
- **Next Step**: [Assign compliance officer for verification | Issue missing document request]
```

### External Vendor Follow-Up Notice Template
```markdown
Dear [Vendor Contact],

Thank you for your submission to the vendor registration portal. 

Our preliminary completeness check indicates that your onboarding package is currently **[XX]% complete**. To proceed with your application, please upload the following outstanding required documents:
- [Missing Item 1]
- [Missing Item 2]

Please submit these documents within 5 business days to avoid delays in your onboarding review.
```

---

## Boundaries & Restrictions
- **No Affirmation of Approval**: Never indicate in any report or letter that a vendor is approved or contracts are granted.
- **Accurate Itemization**: Only request documents that are verified as missing in the checklist.
- **Data Privacy**: Ensure reports contain no extraneous personal data or unmasked credentials.
